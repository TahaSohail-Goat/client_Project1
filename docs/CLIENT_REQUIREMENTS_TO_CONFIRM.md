# Architecture / Interior Design Studio — Remaining Client Approvals

**Confirmed:** This client is an **architecture and interior design company presenting its work**, NOT a real-estate listing agency. Do not ask this question again.

Codex can safely build the approved direction with clearly editable placeholder text. These outstanding questions help finalize truthful presentation and conversion features:

1. **Studio brand:** exact studio/business name, current logo (SVG preferred), tagline, style guide, typography and brand color preferences.
2. **Image grouping / attribution:** which of the 4 exterior, 11 interior, and 3 physical model images are from the same commission? Any project names, dates, locations and credited photographers that may be displayed? May the website publish them publicly?
3. **Core service offerings:** beyond the two confirmed broad domains (architecture and interior design), does the studio offer planning, renovation, material selection, fit-outs, visualizations or project oversight? No claims until approved.
4. **About / process:** approved studio introduction, founders/team bios (if the client wants them), project methodology steps, professional accreditations only if verifiable.
5. **Inquiry contact:** email, phone, WhatsApp or connected form service and consent/privacy text. No success animation or 'message sent' unless a real delivery succeeds.
6. **Site pages:** confirm home + Work + Studio + Contact as base scope, and whether dedicated Services pages or real project case studies are desired. Case studies require approved photo groupings/metadata.
7. **Photographic masters:** request higher-resolution masters where available; exterior source images are only ~1080px wide.
8. **Extra 3D:** confirm whether high-quality **2.5D scroll illusions** are sufficient. A genuine house orbit, explorable interiors or construction visualization requires separately supplied 3D geometry (GLB, SketchUp, Blender, Revit/CAD) or separately scoped modeling.
9. **Deployment and content management:** domain/hosting, language, CMS edits, forms/CRM, analytics approval, SEO targets and launch/revision deadline.
10. **Visual approval:** client sign-off for hero facade, ivory reveal, interior scroll stack, model-photo 3D plane treatment and mobile motion fallback.

**Launch blockers:** image-use permission, truthful studio identity/contact information, agreed page scope, and working inquiry destination. The frontend can be prototyped before these are delivered.
